The NA slider spans 0.01–0.30, but equivalent NA may fall below 0.01 when the beam waist is large.

Key lesson: beam waist and illumination NA cannot be chosen independently. At a given wavelength, choosing one automatically chooses the other.
What changes?
A very thin waist gives excellent sectioning locally, but the useful propagation distance shrinks rapidly.
The question to ask
“Can a sheet X µm thick interrogate a FOV of Y width”
The beam waist tells you how thin the sheet is. The Rayleigh length tells you how useful that waist is for imaging.
